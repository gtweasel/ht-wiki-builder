Unused days are counted from the later of the player's arrival date or last played match.

Current Value is the sum of smooth skill-value curves for keeper, defending, playmaking, winger, passing, scoring, and set pieces.

Development is a continuous age-in-days curve through age 30. Players age 30 and older receive no future development value.

Usage = 2(-Unused Days / 28)
Usefulness = (Current Value x Usage) + (Development x Usage2)

Injury status, form, salary, TSI, and roster size do not change the score.
