Evaluate Roster

Rank the selected team from least useful to most useful using current HTMS value, development potential through age 30, and how recently each player has been used.

Waiting for team data.

Unused time starts from the more recent of the player's arrival date or last match. Current HTMS is never reduced because of age; players age 30 and older simply receive no additional development potential.

Usage = 2 ^ (-Unused Days / 28)
Overall Usefulness = (HTMS x Usage) + ((HTMS30 - HTMS) x Usage ^ 2)

Lower overall usefulness ranks first. The evaluator does not set a roster-size target or label players keep, sell, or fire.
